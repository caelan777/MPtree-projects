Installing MPTree

Three steps. The second one is Windows being careful, not something wrong.

  1. 1
    Download and open the file

    Click Download for Windows, then open MPTree-Setup-0.1.2.exe from your Downloads.

  2. 2
    If Windows says it protected your PC

    This early release is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows does not know the publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3
    Let it find your music

    MPTree looks through your own folders for songs. To use one folder only, pick it under Settings, Music folder.

What early release means

It plays your music and syncs with your phone. A few things are not there yet.

  1. ✓
    Works today

    Your library, playlists, the equaliser, crossfade, cutting tracks, lyrics, the bin, keyboard and media keys, and the MPTree account: sign in and your songs and playlists travel between this computer and your phone.

  2. …
    Still coming

    Buying Pro on Windows (Pro bought on your phone does arrive here through your account), and updates that install themselves. For now a new version is a new download from this page; the app tells you when there is one.
