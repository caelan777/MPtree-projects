MPTree for Android

MPTree for Android comes from Google Play, which keeps it up to date for you.

Get it on Google Play

Got MPTree from this website before?

That copy no longer gets updates. The one on Google Play is signed differently, so Android will not install it on top: the old one goes first. Your music files stay where they are, and a backup brings back your playlists, likes and settings.

  1. 1
    Make a backup

    In MPTree, open Settings, then Backup & Restore, and export a backup.

  2. 2
    Uninstall the old MPTree

    Hold its icon and choose Uninstall.

  3. 3
    Install it from Google Play

    Tap Get it on Google Play above, then Install.

  4. 4
    Restore your backup

    Open MPTree, go to Settings, then Restore backup, and pick the backup you made.
