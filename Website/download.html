Got MPTree for Android from this website before?

That copy no longer gets updates, and Android will not install the Google Play one on top of it. Your music stays where it is.

  1. In MPTree, open Settings, then Backup & Restore, and make a backup.
  2. Uninstall the old MPTree.
  3. Install MPTree from Google Play.
  4. Open it, go to Settings, then Restore backup.
