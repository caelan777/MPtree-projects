Free music player for Android

Your music.
On your device.

MPTree plays the music files already on your phone. No account, no subscription, no internet. It is free, and nothing you listen to ever leaves your device.

Download for Android Windows Coming soon

Free APK download. Works on Android 7.0 and newer.

What it does

  • Works offline

    Plays the files on your phone. No connection needed, ever.

  • Automatic playlists

    Favourites, Recently Played, Most Played and Last Added keep themselves up to date.

  • Equaliser and crossfade

    Adjust the sound, fade between tracks, change playback speed.

  • Cut tracks

    Trim a song and save it as a new file. The original stays as it is.

  • Add music from the web

    A built in browser for getting new files onto your phone.

  • Backups

    Save your playlists, artwork and music to one file, and restore it later.

How it looks

Black and white, with colour only where it means something.

Nothing is uploaded

There is no account, no tracking and no server. Your music and what you listen to stay on your phone.

Get MPTree

Free, for Android 7.0 or newer. Installing takes about a minute.

Download for Android Windows Coming soon
  1. 1
    Download the app

    Tap the button. Your phone saves the MPTree.apk file.

  2. 2
    Open the file

    Tap it in your downloads. Android may ask to allow installing from your browser. Allow it, this is normal for apps outside the Play Store.

  3. 3
    Let it find your music

    Open MPTree and allow access to your audio files. Your library appears, and you are ready to play.
