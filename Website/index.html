What it does

  • Works offline

    Plays the files on your phone. No connection needed, ever.

  • Automatic playlists

    Favourites, Recently Played, Most Played and Last Added keep themselves up to date.

  • Equaliser and crossfade

    Adjust the sound, fade between tracks, change playback speed.

  • Cut tracks

    Trim a song and save it as a new file. The original stays as it is.

  • Add music from the web

    A built in browser for getting new files onto your phone.

  • Backups

    Save your playlists, artwork and music to one file, and restore it later.

How it looks
