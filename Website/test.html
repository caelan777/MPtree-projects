Test build, not a release

MPTree Pro, to try

MPTree with Pro in it. Here Pro costs nothing: one tap unlocks it and one tap locks it again, so every part of it can be tried before it is on sale.

Download the test build

If MPTree came from Google Play on your phone, Android will not put this over it: the two are signed differently. Make a backup first (Settings, Export backup), uninstall MPTree, install this, then restore the backup. A copy from this website updates in place and keeps everything.

What to try

All of it is in Settings. This is test build 2.

  1. 1
    Personalise, without Pro

    Settings, then Personalise. The switch at the top picks dark or light mode: each has its own colour and header card. Everything but plain black and white has a lock, and tapping a lock opens MPTree Pro.

  2. 2
    Unlock Pro

    Get Pro, then Unlock Pro for free. The locks go.

  3. 3
    Colours and the header card

    Try purple in dark mode, pink or sage in light mode, and give the header card a colour of its own, even one from the other mode.

  4. 4
    Records

    Pick one, then open the player. Tap the record while a song with a cover plays: the cover goes on it. Tap again for the record.

  5. 5
    App icon

    Pick Light, Record or Stamp, then look at your home screen. It can take a few seconds, and some phones move the icon to the app drawer. Your photo adds a second icon with a picture of your choice.

  6. 6
    Clean up non-songs

    Settings, Library. Play any of them with the button on the right to hear what it is. Untick what you want to keep; the rest goes to the bin.

  7. 7
    Backup

    Settings, Export backup. Turn off Save everything to pick: all songs, some songs or none, the bin, playlists, covers, likes and settings. Restoring a backup with only some of these leaves the rest alone. This is not part of Pro.

  8. 8
    Lock it again

    Open MPTree Pro and tap Lock Pro again. Everything goes back to black and white, and the icon to the classic one.
