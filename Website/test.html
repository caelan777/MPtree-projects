Test build, not a release

MPTree Pro, to try

MPTree with Pro in it. Here Pro costs nothing: one tap unlocks it and one tap locks it again, so every part of it can be tried before it is on sale.

Download the test build

If MPTree came from Google Play on your phone, Android will not put this over it: the two are signed differently. Make a backup first (Settings, Export backup), uninstall MPTree, install this, then restore the backup. A copy from this website updates in place and keeps everything.

What to try

All of it is in Settings. This is test build 15.

  1. 1
    Personalise, without Pro

    Settings, then Personalise. Dark and Light at the top of the frame pick the mode: colour, header card and record are kept for each mode on its own, the app icon is one for both. A row with an arrow on the right has more; swipe or tap the arrow. Tap anything, locked or not: it shows straight away, and the header card above the sheet stays in view. Close the sheet and the locked picks go back.

  2. 2
    A free week

    Get Pro, then Try it free for 7 days. Pro is on, and the Pro card in Settings counts down. End the free week stops it as if the week had run out: everything goes back, and a note says so once. Reset the free week lets you start it again.

  3. 3
    Unlock Pro

    Get Pro, then Unlock Pro for free. What you were trying stays.

  4. 4
    Colours and the header card

    Try AMOLED and graphite in dark mode, paper, stone, pink and sage in light. Give the header card a colour of its own.

  5. 5
    Records

    Pick one, then open the player. Tap the record while a song with a cover plays: the cover goes on it. Tap again for the record.

  6. 6
    App icon

    Pick Light, Record or Stamp, then look at your home screen. Your photo adds a second icon with a picture of your choice.

  7. 7
    Clean up non-songs

    Settings, Library. Play any of them with the button on the right. Untick what you want to keep; the rest goes to the bin.

  8. 8
    Your MPTree account (new)

    Unlock Pro, then Settings, Account & sync, Sign in with Google. A switch appears in the top card: This device is what you had, All devices is one library for all your phones. Put a like or a playlist in All devices and check that This device stays as it was. Give All devices another look in Personalise, then switch back and forth.

    No second phone? At the bottom of the account page, Add a test phone. Its three songs come in within a minute or two (All devices only). Then Test phone makes a playlist, and Test phone deletes its songs for good: they land in the bin of All devices. Delete one for good yourself and look in the bin under Deleted for good here.

  9. 9
    Backup

    Settings, Export backup. Everything is ticked; untick what you do not want, or tap the song count to pick songs. This is not part of Pro.

  10. 10
    Lock it again

    Open MPTree Pro and tap Lock Pro again. Everything goes back to black and white, and the icon to the classic one.
