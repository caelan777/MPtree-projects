MPTree Pro, to try

MPTree with Pro in it. Here Pro costs nothing: one tap unlocks it and one tap locks it again, so every part of it can be tried before it is on sale.

Download the test build

If MPTree came from Google Play on your phone, Android will not put this over it: the two are signed differently. Make a backup first (Settings, Export backup), uninstall MPTree, install this, then restore the backup. A copy from this website updates in place and keeps everything.

What to try

All of it is in Settings.

  1. 1
    Personalise, before unlocking

    Settings, then Personalise. Tap a shade, a header card or a record: it shows straight away, everywhere. Close the sheet and it should all go back.

  2. 2
    Unlock Pro

    Tap Keep it, or Get MPTree Pro at the top of Settings, then Unlock Pro for free. What you were trying stays.

  3. 3
    Records

    Pick one, then open the player. The picture disc needs a song with a cover.

  4. 4
    App icon

    Pick Light, Record or Stamp, then look at your home screen. It can take a few seconds, and some phones move the icon to the app drawer. Your photo adds a second icon with a picture of your choice.

  5. 5
    Clean up non-songs

    Settings, Library. It lists voice notes, recordings and anything under a minute. Untick what you want to keep; the rest goes to the bin, where Undo and Restore still work.

  6. 6
    Lock it again

    Open MPTree Pro and tap Lock Pro again. Everything should go back to the free look.
