MPTree

Test build, not a release

MPTree Pro, to try

MPTree with Pro in it. Here Pro costs nothing: one tap unlocks it and one tap locks it again, so every part of it can be tried before it is on sale.

Download the test build

If MPTree came from Google Play on your phone, Android will not put this over it: the two are signed differently. Make a backup first (Settings, Backup & restore), uninstall MPTree, install this, then restore the backup. A copy from this website updates in place and keeps everything.

What to try

All of it is in Settings. This is test build 78, for 1.3.3.

  1. 78
    New in 78: Halloween, and when your day of Pro ends

    Tap the settings button. Halloween comes out of the dark, then a treat: a day of Pro, or 24 hours more if you already have a day. In this test build it is Halloween every day, once each time you open MPTree; on Google Play it is from 28 October to 1 November, once. Then watch the ad for a day of Pro and, on the Pro page, tap End the 24 hours (test build): MPTree asks for another ad. Not now keeps Pro for one more hour, the second Not now turns it off.

  2. 77
    New in 77: selecting, and Next up

    Hold a song in a playlist to select it: the page looks like Songs now, with one bar at the top instead of two. Pick a few and tap Edit: the sheet opens above the bars, and a cover you choose and crop stays. On Songs, the sort button can no longer be tapped while you select. Open the player and tap Next up: it comes up only as high as the player. Scroll down the list, then pull the top of the page down: it closes from anywhere.

  3. 76
    New in 76: the player as it was

    Open the player. The record is back at its old size and the player comes up from the bottom only as high as it needs, with your list dimmed above it; tap there to close it. Push up, or tap Next up, and the whole list takes the screen.

  4. 75
    New in 75: the logo

    Tap the logo to fold the header and the player away, then scroll the songs: scrolling still folds and unfolds the header after a tap, it no longer stops. Tap it again to bring everything back. The logo stays in the top left: it can no longer be dragged around the screen. Holding it still opens the switch for automatic folding.

  5. 74
    New in 74: the player fits

    Open the player. Its top, and the top of Next up, start under the clock and the icons now, not behind them. The record takes the room that is left, so the next song sits at the very bottom with no empty space under it. Try it with Settings, Size on each of the three: nothing should be cut off, and nothing should be left empty.

  6. 73
    New in 73: the player, again

    The player has its rounded top and the card with the border back. Under the bar is the next song with Skip, as before. Push up on the player, or tap Next up, and the whole list comes up as a page of its own; pull it down at its top to go back. Pull the player down from almost anywhere to close it.

    Repeat goes round: off, the whole list, this song (a small 1 on the icon). Try the whole list with the screen off: after the last song the first should come.

    Remove in the player keeps it open, and the next song from the same list plays.

    Hold a song in a playlist: the bar along the top and bottom should look like the rest of MPTree now, with no player showing behind it.

    Size in Settings: Small, Medium and Large are further apart.

    Playlists in your own order: tap Reorder next to My playlists, or hold a playlist, then drag it by the dots. Done or Back stops it.

    Scroll down the songs: only the header folds away, the player stays. Scroll up a little and the header comes back.

  7. 72
    New in 72: the player, full screen

    Play a song and open the player. It takes the whole screen now; the arrow at the top left, or dragging the top bar down, closes it. Under the buttons is one bar with speed, like, share and remove.

    Scroll down for Next up: the song that is on, then everything that comes after it, up to 500 songs, with where it comes from on the right. Tap a song to play it. Hold the dots on the left of a song and drag it to another place: tell me if it moves where your finger goes, and if it then plays in that order.

    In the lists, the song that is on is a rounded tile now, without the line on the left.

    Playlists: play a song from a playlist. It plays there: the Songs page does not mark it or jump to it, and the playlist's cover shows moving bars (still bars when paused). Then play something from Songs and check the bars are gone from the playlist.

  8. 71
    New in 71: the icon, quietly

    Pick another icon in Personalise, Icons, and tap Apply. Nothing asks anything and nothing closes. Go to your home screen: the new icon is there. Tell me if it is not, or if it left the home screen.

  9. 70
    New in 70: small phones

    On a small phone with large text, three things no longer break. Try it with Settings, Size on Large, and your phone's own text size turned up.

    Settings: rows with Free with an ad (Account & sync, Personalise, Clean up) show it under their text, so the text uses the whole row instead of one word a line.

    Personalise: on a page of its own, after you pick something locked, the button in the corner says Keep it with "Free with an ad" small under it, and no longer covers the back arrow.

    The search bar stays inside the card at the top.

  10. 65
    New since 1.2.9

    Personalise is laid out like Android's own Wallpaper & style. The two screens at the top are large, and get smaller as you scroll down; they no longer make the page jump on the way back up, and the page ends where its last row does. Under them, Custom styles: Aviela and Lantern side by side, with Take the style off while one is on. Then one row each for Background, Colours, Header card, Icons and Records; a row opens its choices over the whole page, and the arrow goes back. Aviela's and Lantern's own pages keep their choices in rows you swipe sideways.

    Background: pick a photo, place it, and set how strongly it shows. It goes behind the whole app; Aviela's flowers and Lantern's light stay on top. It stays on the phone, not in the account or a backup.

    Icons are back, with two new ones for Aviela and Lantern; putting on Aviela or Lantern brings its icon. Since 71 it changes the next time you leave MPTree, without asking.

    Lock screen (new in 65): with a song playing, the notification on the lock screen and in the status bar shows the MPTree mark instead of a dot.

    Black mode (new in 65): the Pro page, Account & sync and Personalise are a dark grey instead of pure black, and grey text is easier to read.

    Picking songs (new in 64): hold a song. The header makes way for a plain bar with a back arrow, how many are picked and Select all; along the bottom are Like, Add to, Play next, Share, Shuffle, Edit and, last and in red, Delete, which asks first. Try sharing three songs to WhatsApp. In a playlist it works the same way.

    Backup & restore is one row now, which asks which of the two.

    Without Pro, Pro things now say Free with an ad instead of PRO.

    From the second day, after ten songs, Google's own review card may come up once. In a test build Google never really shows it, so nothing should happen at all; just check the app does not close.

  11. 1
    Apply

    In Personalise nothing is kept until you tap Apply at the bottom. Change something and leave with the cross or the back button: it asks whether to throw it away, or keep editing.

  12. 2
    Everything, once

    New, and the one that matters most. This build is made in a new way that leaves unused code out, which makes it less than half the size. If a rule is missing, something stops working. So go round once: play a song with the screen off, scan for music, sign in, turn on Pro, let two devices sync, make a backup, put the widget on the home screen. Anything that does nothing or closes the app: tell me which.

  13. 3
    Personalise, without Pro

    Settings, then Personalise. The two screens at the top are the real app and the real player, and they change with everything you tap. Styles has whole looks, each with its own record. Two of them have a page of their own: tap one and it opens, and the arrow at the top goes back. Aviela is pink with flowers: thirteen flower records, ten ways for the app to wear flowers, and a header card in white, pink or black. Lantern is green light: six records, four header cards, and the colour of the app, which is black unless you pick another. New: its records are drawn again, and it lights the play button and the chosen tab too. New: shuffle is pink in Aviela and green in Lantern, and Lantern is only on its own page, no longer under Custom. Custom has every choice on its own. New: picking something there while Aviela or Lantern is on takes that whole style off first. Also new: Settings has no dark and light switch any more, and a song's edit screen has no genre. A colour is dark or light, and picking one puts the app in that mode. Tap anything, locked or not: it shows straight away. Close the page and the locked picks go back.

  14. 4
    Pro for a day, with an ad (new)

    Since build 58 the free week is gone. Open the Pro page: the black card at the top of Settings, or anything Pro (Clean up non-songs, or a Pro record in Personalise and then Apply). It shows two cards of the same size: For good, €2,99, and At no cost, 24 hours. Tap Watch ad. In the EU Google first asks whether ads may be personal; either answer works. A test ad from Google plays; watch it to the end and Pro is on for 24 hours. Close it early and nothing changes. With no internet it says so. New in this build: at the top of the Pro page is a short film about Pro. Tap it: it plays full screen with sound, also without internet, and the cross takes you back. Since build 60 the Pro page fills the whole screen, like Account & sync. Since build 59 both cards show however you open the page, also from Settings, and the Pro card in Settings says Free for a day with an ad, or pay once. End the 24 hours stops it again. In the EU, Settings now also has Privacy choices for ads under Help.

  15. 5
    Unlock Pro

    Get Pro, then Unlock (test build) on the For good card. What you were trying stays.

  16. 6
    Colours and the header card

    Try graphite in dark mode, paper, stone, pink and sage in light. Give the header card a colour of its own. Since build 57: MPTree opens plain white the first time again; the black header to start with is gone.

  17. 7
    Records

    Pick one, then open the player. Tap the record while a song with a cover plays: the cover goes on it. Tap again for the record.

  18. 8
    Crop a cover

    Open a song's menu, then Photo, and pick a photo that is not square. A window comes up: drag the photo, zoom with the slider or two fingers, then Use this. The ring shows what fits on the record's label.

  19. 9
    Shuffle and repeat

    New: the full player has a shuffle button on the left and a repeat button on the right, and the speed is in the row with Like and Share. Play a song from a playlist and tap shuffle: it should only take songs from that playlist. New in this build: repeat is a switch of its own. Tap it with shuffle on and shuffle stays on; the song that is on plays again when it ends, with the crossfade if you have one set. Next still goes to the next song, also from the lock screen. Put a song on Play next first and it keeps its place through all of it. And skip a few songs in a row: the app should not stutter any more.

    Since build 56: set the crossfade to 8 seconds and let a few songs run out. The last 8 seconds should fade into the next song, with no silence and without the next song starting over from the beginning.

  20. 10
    Lantern's question, and Aviela's lily

    New. With Pro on and the sound up, tap Lantern under Styles. The screen goes dark and something builds: a rising drone and a heart that beats faster. Then a hit, the green light of a man's shoulders comes on in the dark, and a voice asks whether you are afraid. It asks once only. Slide the knob left for Yes: the light goes out and Lantern locks for 7 days. Slide it right for No: Lantern opens out of green light. In this test build a tap on the locked tile opens it again and it will ask again. Then tap Aviela: a pink lily opens where you tapped, and there is a lily record. The figure is on the question, and behind the list while Lantern is on; Lantern's colour is black, white or paper, with paper first. On paper and white he shows faint, as if through the page. The light comes up slowly out of the dark. New in this build: a test build asks every time Lantern is put on (pick another style and Apply first), and under Custom the free colours come first.

  21. 11
    Clean up non-songs

    Settings, Library. Play any of them with the button on the right. Untick what you want to keep; the rest goes to the bin.

  22. 12
    Your MPTree account (new)

    Unlock Pro, then Settings, Account & sync, Sign in with Google. Your playlists, likes and settings go into your account, and every device you sign in on shows the same. Tap a device name on the account page to rename it. Signing out on one device leaves the others signed in, and asks Android only once to delete songs. Songs a device had already are not sent to it again; if some are there twice, the account page offers to delete the extra copies.

    No second device? At the bottom of the account page, Add a test device. Its three songs come in within a minute or two. Then Test device makes a playlist, and Test device deletes its songs permanently: they land in your bin. Delete one of them permanently yourself, then look in the bin under Permanently deleted. Sign out at the end: you can choose to delete the songs that came from your other devices.

    Since build 57: the account page is new, and since this build it fills the whole screen. Your devices stand in a triangle, this one on top, and a free place is drawn dashed. While songs go from one device to another, dots run along the line between them, in the direction they go; when everything is in step, the dots go slowly both ways. Under it one plain sentence says what is going on and what to do. Add a test device and watch its songs come in. Add or remove devices and What syncs, and what does not fold open.

    Since build 55: signing in again keeps what happened while you were away. Sign out on one device, put songs in the bin on another and delete them permanently, then sign in on the first again: they stay in the bin there and do not come back.

  23. 13
    Backup

    Settings, Export backup. Everything is ticked; untick what you do not want, or tap the song count to pick songs. This is not part of Pro.

  24. 14
    Cutting a song (new)

    Hold a song, then Cut. Drag the two grips to the part you want to keep and tap Listen. Save it, then play the cut: it should start and stop where you put the grips, also with the screen off.

    Since build 57: cut an MP3, then delete the original song. The cut should keep its own name and its own length, not turn into the whole song. A cut made with an older build stays wrong; cut it again.

  25. 15
    Spanish (new)

    Settings, Language. Spanish is there next to English and Dutch.

  26. 16
    Lock it again

    Open MPTree Pro and tap Lock Pro again. Everything goes back to black and white.
