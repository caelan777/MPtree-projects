Privacy Policy

MPTree collects nothing and uploads nothing. Your music and what you listen to never leave your device.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to the MPTree Android app and the website at mp-tree.net. MPTree is an offline music player built by Verkuijl. There is no company behind it and no server that stores your information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind, and it never sends us your files, your playlists, or what you listen to. We have no way of knowing who you are or what you play, because none of that information is ever sent anywhere. The one request the app makes on its own is the update check described under Internet access below.

That is the app. The website is a separate thing with its own answer, and it is written out under The website below.

Access to your music files

To play your music, MPTree asks Android for permission to read the audio files on your device (the Read audio or Read storage permission). This access is used only to find and play your music on the device itself. Your files are never sent to us or shared with anyone. The one time they leave the phone is when you sign in to an MPTree account yourself: then they go to your own other phones. If you deny this permission, MPTree simply cannot show your library.

Internet access

MPTree plays your local files and does not need the internet to work. It never streams, and never sends us your listening activity, your music or anything about it.

There is one background request, and only in the copy you download from this website: about once a day, when you open the app, it fetches a small file from mp-tree.net to see whether a newer version has been released. That request carries nothing except the ask itself. Like any web request, including loading this page, it does reach the server with your device's IP address. Nothing is stored against you, and nothing links one check to the next. The Google Play version does not make this request at all, and neither does the demo you can try on this site.

MPTree does not fetch lyrics. Tapping Search online on a song opens your own browser on a web search for it, the same as any other outward link: from that point you are in your browser, not in MPTree, and what happens there is between you and the site you land on. Lyrics you paste into the app stay on your phone.

Apart from that, MPTree opens a connection only when you do something that needs one: tapping an outward link, which opens in your own browser, buying or restoring Pro, which goes through Google Play, or signing in to an MPTree account, which talks to your own Google Drive and your own other phones.

MPTree Pro

MPTree Pro is an optional one-time purchase, sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details, your name or your email address; Google Play only tells the app whether your Google account owns Pro, and the app remembers that answer on your phone. Everything you set up with Pro, like colours, the record and the header card, stays on your phone too.

Pro can also be tried for free for one week. That week is counted on your phone and nowhere else: no account, no payment details, and nothing is charged when it ends.

The MPTree account

With Pro you can sign in with your Google account, on up to three phones. This is optional: MPTree works the same without it.

What is saved, and where. Your playlists, likes, play counts, the names, artists, lyrics and covers you set, the bin, cut tracks, and your settings are saved in your own Google Drive, in its app folder. That is a hidden folder that only MPTree can open; it cannot see the rest of your Drive, and your other files cannot see it. It sits in your Drive, under your Google account, not with us. We have no server and never receive a copy. To sign in, MPTree asks Google for access to that one folder and nothing else. It reads your name and email address from Google only to show you which account you are signed in with, on your phone.

Your songs. The songs themselves are not saved in your account. They go from one of your phones straight to your other phones, so each one has them all. To find each other, the phones leave a short note in the same app folder, and ask a Google server (a so-called STUN server) which internet address they have, the way video calls do. When a phone that needs a song is not open, the phone that has it can leave the song in your Drive's app folder instead; the other phone deletes it from there as soon as it has it. So a song can sit in your own Drive for a while, and it counts towards your Drive's storage while it does. Songs only move on wifi unless you allow mobile data.

Stopping. Signing out stops syncing on that phone and leaves everything on it as it is. To delete what the account holds, open your Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, and choose Delete hidden app data next to MPTree. You can also take away MPTree's access at myaccount.google.com, under Security.

Backups

MPTree can save a backup of your playlists, artwork, and music to a single file. That file is written to a location you choose on your own device or storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party. You are in full control of where it goes and who can see it.

The website

The mp-tree.net website is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies. There is no form anywhere on it, so there is nothing here that can submit your text to a server. Reaching us means sending an email yourself, from your own mail app.

Cloudflare, as the host, keeps a running count of the requests it serves for this site: how many pages were loaded, roughly which country they were loaded from, and which browser asked. We look at those counts to see whether anyone is finding the app. They are totals rather than people. There is no tracking script on any page here, nothing is stored in your browser, no profile is built, and none of it is sold or handed on. If you loaded this page, you are one anonymous tick in a daily total, and that is the whole of it.

Third parties

There are none. The site used to carry a comment section run by Cusdis, which stored what you typed on their servers rather than ours. That service went down and has been removed, along with everything it loaded. No page here now fetches anything from another company, and nothing you do on this site is sent anywhere except to the server handing you the page.

Children

MPTree is a general audience music player and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

This page describes MPTree as it works today. If a future version adds something that handles your data differently, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new date at the top, and it will say plainly what changed.
