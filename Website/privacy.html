Privacy Policy

MPTree collects nothing and uploads nothing. Your music and what you listen to never leave your device.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to the MPTree Android app and the website at mp-tree.net. MPTree is an offline music player built by Caelan Verkuijl. There is no company behind it and no server that stores your information.

What MPTree collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We have no way of knowing who you are or what you listen to, because none of that information is ever sent anywhere.

Access to your music files

To play your music, MPTree asks Android for permission to read the audio files on your device (the Read audio or Read storage permission). This access is used only to find and play your music on the device itself. Your files are never uploaded, copied off the device, or shared with anyone. If you deny this permission, MPTree simply cannot show your library.

Internet access

MPTree plays your local files and does not need the internet to work. The only time it opens a connection is when you tap an outward link yourself, such as the optional support page, which opens in your own browser. MPTree does not contact any server of ours, send us your activity, stream anything, or phone home in the background.

Backups

MPTree can save a backup of your playlists, artwork, and music to a single file. That file is written to a location you choose on your own device or storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party. You are in full control of where it goes and who can see it.

The website

The mp-tree.net website is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The feedback form on the site opens your own email app with a message ready to send. It does not submit your text to any server.

Comments on the website

The comment section on the homepage is provided by Cusdis, a third party, and runs entirely in your browser. Anything you type there, including the name you give, is stored by Cusdis rather than by us, and is public once posted. You do not need an account to comment, and the widget is not loaded until that part of the page comes into view. Nothing about the app itself is sent through it. If you would rather not use it, simply do not post.

Children

MPTree is a general audience music player and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

This page describes MPTree as it works today. If a future version adds something that handles your data differently, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new date at the top, and it will say plainly what changed.
