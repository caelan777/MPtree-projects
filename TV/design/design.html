A home screen for the TV, in MPTree white

Four screens at real TV size (1920×1080), shown at half. Everything moves with the remote: the item in focus turns black with white type and grows a little, the way the accent inverts in the app. Top: account and Home on the left, Apps, Settings and Screensaver on the right. Middle: the clock, MPTree numbers and your Home Assistant things. Bottom: only your four apps and "Add app". All numbers and Home Assistant names here are examples.
