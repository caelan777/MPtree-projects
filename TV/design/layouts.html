Now with heating and the alarm. Heating shows the big radiator's set temperature; with focus on it, left and right change it by half a degree, OK turns it on or off. The alarm is a switch for your Wednesday and Thursday 08:00 alarm. One button, two states. The tile says ON or nothing, and the line under it says what OK will do next. It turns on by itself at sunset (your automation), and you can always turn it off or on again from the couch. A short message at the top confirms the press.
