Ten home screens for the living room TV

Every screen holds the same things so you can compare them fairly: account, Home and Apps side by side, Settings and Screensaver; your Sunset button and All lights off; inside temperature (living room sensor) and the weather outside; the MPTree numbers; and the dock with YouTube, NPO Start, HBO Max, Browser, MPTree and Add app. The item in black is where the remote's focus is. Numbers are examples. App tiles are stand-ins; the real ones use each app's own picture.
