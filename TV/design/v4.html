MPTree TV · round four · design only · 1 Nov 2026

The same five Homes, made more beautiful

Each layout now has one idea it is built around: the clock as the picture, a magazine cover, the chosen app filling the room, a window onto the sky, and the day as one drawing. Underneath they keep what round three made clear: words on buttons, ON and OFF apart from what OK does, and the line at the top. The sixth screen is Home with Home Assistant switched off. The yellow circles are notes for you, not part of the screen.
