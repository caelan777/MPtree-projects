# MPTree 0.1.0 beta

The first public build of MPTree, an offline music player for Android. It plays the
music already on your device. No account, no internet, nothing uploaded.

## Install

1. Download `MPTree-v0.1.0-beta.apk` below.
2. Open it. Android will ask permission to install from your browser or files app. Allow it.
3. On first launch, grant access to your audio files so MPTree can find your music.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

## In this build

- Plays your on-device library, fully offline.
- Automatic playlists: Favourites, Recently Played, Most Played, Last Added.
- Equaliser, crossfade and adjustable playback speed.
- Cut a track and save the trimmed part as a new file, losslessly.
- Add music from the web through a built in browser.
- Back up and restore your playlists, artwork and music as one archive.
- Lock screen controls and a home screen widget.

## Good to know

- This is an early beta. If something breaks, there is a feedback box on the website.
- It installs as its own app. If you used a much older build under the previous name,
  this one starts with an empty library. Export a backup from the old app and restore it
  here to carry your playlists across.
- Nothing is collected or uploaded. Music you add through the built in browser is your
  own responsibility.
