MPTree for Android · design proposal · 30 Oct 2026

Discover: free music you can keep

A third tab next to Songs and Playlists. You browse and search free music by independent artists, play a 30 second preview, tap the download button, and the mp3 is saved to Music/MPTree, where it shows up in Songs like any other file. Sources: Audius first, the Internet Archive second (see discover-sources.md).

Where it livesA third tab in the header card: Songs, Playlists, Discover. On a 412 phone it has its label; on a 360 phone only the compass, because three labels do not fit next to the logo and the settings button.
Who it is forThe people who install MPTree and have no music files. An empty Songs list now sends them to Discover instead of leaving them stuck.
Being honestFirst visit shows one dismissible card: these are independent artists who chose to share, not the charts. The licence is on every row, the source under the list.
ColourOnly black and white. The selected genre, the preview bar and the finished download all use the accent. No violet, it stays for shuffle and selection.

Tap the cover to preview, tap the row for details. A preview is 30 seconds, streamed, never saved. The row shows the time and a thin bar in the accent. If you were listening to your own music it pauses, and picks up again when the preview ends. A track whose artist did not allow downloads is marked "Preview only" with a dashed button; by default the list hides those.

The button turns into its own progress ring. No percentage, the ring and the megabytes say enough. Tapping the ring cancels. A second tap elsewhere queues the next one, one download at a time. When it finishes the button fills with the accent and shows a check, and the line under the title says "In your Songs". Downloads keep going when you leave the tab or lock the phone (a native job, like playback).

The fix for "I can't find any songs!" Today an empty library is a dead end. This screen says why it is empty, and gives two ways out: Discover for people without files, a rescan for people who have them on a computer.

Open questions for Caelan

Free or Pro? Proposal: Discover is free for everyone. It is how new people get their first songs, so locking it would undo the point.

Mobile data. Proposal: downloads over mobile data ask once ("Download on mobile data? 6.1 MB"), with a "Wi-Fi only" switch in Settings.

Hide the tab. A switch in Settings to hide Discover for people who only want their own files, so the app stays as quiet as it is today.
