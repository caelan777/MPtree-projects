BRAND SYSTEM
V0.1.0 / 2026-07-26

Black and white. One mark. A lot of space.

MPTree plays the music already on your device. Nothing streams, nothing uploads, nothing phones home. The identity is built to feel the same way: quiet, exact, and free of anything that is only there to decorate.

There is no brand colour. The accent is simply whichever of black or white is not currently the background, and that inversion is the entire system.

Mark

An MP3 drawn in one line

An M and P form flowing into a rounded 3, set in a single thin stroke with rounded caps. It carries the whole identity, so it is never modified: no gradient, no tint, no glow, no outline, no shadow, no rotation.

Clear space on all sides is one quarter of the mark's width (dashed). It has to stay legible small, so it is tested down to favicon size rather than only admired large:

16px24px32px48px64px96px
One master, everything generated. The artwork lives once, in source/mptree-mark.svg, painted with currentColor. Every icon and favicon in this folder is produced from it by build-assets.mjs, so Android, desktop and the website cannot drift apart.
Grounds

The accent inverts with the background

Dark is home, light is a first class citizen, and neither is an afterthought of the other. On black the primary action is white with black type. On white it is black with white type. Same rule, mirrored.

Neutrals

Nine steps, no hue

Every surface in the product is a step on this ramp. True neutrals, not warmed or cooled, because the artwork on screen supplies all the colour a music player needs.

Signals

Three colours, each meaning one thing

These are the only colours in MPTree, and they are not brand colours. Each is a functional signal with a single fixed meaning. They never appear in marketing, nothing else may borrow them, and a fourth is not to be invented: prefer weight, position or an icon instead.

Type

The platform's own faces

System sans for reading, system mono for labels and values. Nothing is downloaded, so text renders on the first frame, which matters more in an offline player than a signature typeface would.

Shape

Three radii, hairlines instead of shadows

Depth comes from the ground getting darker, not from drop shadows. Borders are a single hairline. Motion is short and eased, 150ms for a control, 250ms for a surface, 400ms for a sheet. Nothing bounces, and reduced motion is always honoured.
