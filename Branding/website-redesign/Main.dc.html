What it does

Cut a track

Trim a song down to the part you actually want and save it as a new file. The original stays untouched.

Works offline

Everything plays straight off your phone, so it keeps working in flight mode or underground.

Playlists that fill themselves

Favourites, Recently Played, Most Played and Last Added update as you listen.

Equaliser and crossfade

Shape the sound, fade one track into the next, speed songs up or slow them down.

Controls everywhere

Skip and pause from the lock screen, the notification shade, or a home screen widget.

Backups

Pack your playlists, artwork and music into one file, then restore the lot on a new phone.
