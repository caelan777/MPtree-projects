What it does

Works offline

Everything plays straight off your phone, so it keeps working in flight mode, underground, or anywhere with no signal.

Playlists that fill themselves

Favourites, Recently Played, Most Played and Last Added update as you listen. You can build your own too.

Equaliser and crossfade

Shape the sound to your taste, fade one track into the next, and speed songs up or slow them down.

Cut a track

Trim a song down to the part you actually want and save it as a new file. The original stays untouched.

Controls everywhere

Skip and pause from your lock screen, the notification shade, or a widget on your home screen.

Backups

Pack your playlists, artwork and music into one file, then restore the lot on a new phone.
