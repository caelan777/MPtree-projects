# The paper film

A second ad, forty seconds, told in cut paper. Someone wants to hear a song.
Every app they open drops something on top of it. They keep looking. They find
MPTree, and nothing lands.

The first ad ([script.md](script.md)) argues. This one does not argue with
anyone: it just shows you the thing you already know, and then takes it away.

---

## 1. Why paper

An ad is a sheet that arrives on top of what you were doing. In paper that is
not a metaphor, it is what happens on the table. You see it come down, you see
it cover something, and when it happens eight times you see the pile. Nothing
has to be explained.

It also buys the ending. Once the audience has watched four sheets land, they
brace for the fifth. Not sending it is the whole film.

And it cannot look machine made, which is the point. Paper has fibres, the
edges are never quite straight, and the shadows move a little between frames.
Leave all of that in.

## 2. The look

Black paper, white paper, one mid grey. Nothing else.

Shot from directly above a table, flat on, so every layer reads as a layer and
the small gap between them throws a real shadow. One lamp, off to one side and
low, so the shadows are long enough to see. Never move the lamp.

Everything is a cut shape. The phone is a white rectangle on a black one. The
person is a figure the size of your hand, seen from above, with a separate arm
that moves to tap. The headphones are two circles and a strip.

Do not stabilise the wobble in post. It is the only thing telling the viewer a
person made this.

## 4. The one beat not to cut

0:30.0, the two seconds where nothing lands.

Everything before it is setup for that pause. Every version of this that gets
tightened will want those two seconds back, and every version that takes them
is worse. If the film has to lose time, take it out of the montage at 0:15,
where three sheets do the job of six.

## 5. What the sheets say

Keep them plain and keep them yours. Cut from black paper, white letters
knocked out:

```
AD            SKIP IN 5        GET PREMIUM
AD 1 OF 3     CONTINUE?        UPGRADE TO REMOVE ADS
```

**No real app is named, drawn or hinted at.** No logo anybody owns, no colour
scheme anybody owns, no shape that reads as a particular company. The film is
about what happens, not about who did it, and the moment it points at a
competitor it stops being a good film and starts being a legal problem.

The eight app icons at 0:14 are the same rule: eight different silhouettes,
none of them anybody's.

## 6. Sound

Paper carries this. Record it properly and the film needs almost nothing else.

The ads are dry and loud: a stiff sheet of card dropped flat onto a hard table,
close mic, no reverb. Every slap on the same note so they stack into a rhythm.
The tear at 0:09 is a real tear. The sweep at 0:26 is a hand dragging card
across card in one pass.

The music is the opposite. Nothing plays until 0:32, and when it comes in it
comes in from silence, not from a fade.

**Use the score from the first film.** It is generated in
[ad.html](ad.html) rather than licensed, so it clears with nobody, and the two
ads sounding related is worth having. It is A minor at 87.8 BPM. Take the last
bars, from the endcard, and let the film resolve on them.

## 7. Voiceover, if you want one

It plays with no voice at all, and I would try that cut first. If you want a
voice, three lines is the most it can take:

| At | Line |
|---|---|
| 0:23.0 | You just wanted to hear a song. |
| 0:31.0 | MPTree plays what is already on your phone. Nothing else. |
| 0:39.0 | M P Tree. On Google Play. |

Flat delivery. Not warm, not selling. The film is already doing the feeling.

## 8. How to make it

**Shoot it.** Black card, white card, a craft knife, a phone on a tripod
pointing straight down, one lamp. 12 frames a second, two frames per position,
which is 480 frames and about 240 photographs for the whole film. That is an
afternoon, and it will look like nothing else in the Play Store.

Lock the exposure and the white balance before the first frame. Auto anything
will strobe across the cut.

**Or build it flat.** The same layout in any 2D tool, with a real paper scan as
the texture on every shape and a 12 fps step so it holds two frames at a time.
It loses the shadow and it loses the wobble, so put both back by hand or it
reads as clip art.

**Or generate it**, and expect to fight it. Paper animation is a style video
models think they know, and what they produce is usually smooth, which is the
one thing paper is not. If you go that way, prompt per shot rather than for the
film, and ask for stop motion on twos, visible paper fibre, hard single source
shadow, and no camera move.

Whichever route, the sound is recorded for real. It is half the film and it
costs an hour.

## 9. Cut-downs

**Twenty seconds.** Keep 0:04 to 0:12 (three ads), cut the montage to four
sheets over two seconds, keep the burial, keep the sweep, keep the pause, keep
the end card. The pause does not shrink.

**Six seconds, for a bumper.** One tap, one slap, cut to black, the mark, "No
ads." That is a different film and it works.

**Square and 9:16.** Shot from overhead, so the frame is whatever you crop the
table to. Leave the bottom fifth of the vertical cut empty of anything that
matters: TikTok puts its caption, its username and its buttons there. The first
film handles this with a `--tiktok` flag; here you just leave the room.
